How to read Powder Radar

Mid-mountain modelling. Each forecast is computed for the midpoint between a resort's base and summit elevation, which is where most skiing happens. Summits often receive more, bases less.

Snowfall is a model estimate. Numbers come from global and regional weather models via Open-Meteo. Mountain weather is notoriously local, so treat totals as a ranking signal: a resort showing 40 cm is very likely to beat one showing 5 cm, even if the exact total differs.

The 72-hour column is the most reliable. Forecast skill drops quickly after three days. Use the 7-day number to shortlist and the 72-hour number to commit.

Cold matters. Check the temperatures: snowfall with highs near or above 0 °C at mid-mountain can mean heavy, wet snow or rain lower down.

Forecasts are informational only and are not a substitute for official avalanche bulletins or resort reports. Always check the resort's own conditions page before you travel.
