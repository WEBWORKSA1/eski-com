Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026.

Summary

We collect the minimum data needed to run eSKI, respond to you and show ads that keep the site free. We never sell your personal information.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide: name, email, phone and details you enter in forms (trip quotes, contact, contests, applications, pledges, newsletter).
  • Automatically collected: standard log and device data (IP address, browser, pages visited) collected by our hosting provider and advertising partners.
  • Local storage on your device: preferences such as units, packing checklist progress and cookie consent. These stay in your browser.

How we use it

  • To answer messages and process requests you submit.
  • For trip quote requests, to share your request with up to three relevant travel partners, only with your explicit consent on the form.
  • To run contests and send newsletters or alerts you've signed up for (unsubscribe any time).
  • To operate, secure and improve the site.

Form processing

Form submissions are transmitted securely via a third-party form-handling service (FormSubmit) to our private inbox. They are not published on the site.

Advertising — Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data from partner sites. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), consent for personalised ads is requested through a Google-certified consent message.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded via YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Weather data is fetched from Open-Meteo by your browser; no personal data is sent beyond what's technically required for the request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, India DPDP Act), you may request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, or object to processing. Submit requests through the contact form with the topic "Privacy request".

Retention & security

We keep form data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, then delete it. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online transmission is completely secure.

Children

eSKI is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect their data. Contest entrants must be adults.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above when we do.
