Editorial policy

How we produce, check and correct what you read on eSKI.

Accuracy

Guides are written from established ski-industry knowledge and checked against official resort, pass, avalanche-centre and instructor-association sources. Data that changes often (prices, pass affiliations) is labelled as approximate with a reminder to verify.

Automation

Forecast rankings and news headlines are generated automatically. We disclose the data source on every automated feature.

Independence

Advertisers and partners have no say over editorial content or rankings. Sponsored material is clearly marked "Sponsored".

Corrections

Found an error? Send it via the contact form and we'll fix verified errors promptly.

Safety first

We avoid content that encourages unsafe behaviour and always point readers to professional instruction and official avalanche information.
