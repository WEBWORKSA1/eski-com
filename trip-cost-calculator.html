What actually drives the cost of a ski trip

Lift access is the most variable line. Walk-up day tickets at big-name North American resorts can be several times the price of a pre-purchased multi-day ticket or a multi-resort season pass, while many European and Japanese resorts price six-day passes at a fraction of North American peak rates.

Lodging is usually the biggest single expense. Ski-in/ski-out location adds a premium but can save on parking, shuttles and time. Staying in a valley town and driving up is the classic budget play.

Timing changes everything. Holiday weeks (Christmas–New Year, February school breaks, Presidents' Day, Chinese New Year in Japan) are the most expensive. Early December, mid-January and late March deliver the best value.
