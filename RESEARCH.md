# eSKI.com — Competitive research (35 ski websites)

**Idea chosen:** *eSKI = "everything ski, online"* — a live snow-intelligence + resort-comparison + trip-cost hub whose core monetisation is **(1) AdSense on high-intent travel pages, (2) trip-quote lead generation sold to ski travel specialists, (3) sponsorships/featured resorts, (4) reader support.**

Why this niche: ski travel is a high-CPC advertising vertical (travel, lodging, insurance, gear), visits are habitual in-season (people check snow daily), and a single trip-quote lead is worth far more than a pageview. Live forecast pages create repeat traffic and long-tail SEO ("[resort] snow forecast") without manual writing.

Legend: ✅ = reviewed live during this build (search/fetch, Nov 2026) · 📚 = benchmarked from the site's well-known public structure.
