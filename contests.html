Official rules (summary)

  • No purchase necessary. Purchase does not increase chances of winning. Void where prohibited.
  • Open to entrants who are 18+ or the age of majority in their place of residence.
  • One entry per person per contest. Entries must be original and owned by the entrant; people shown must consent to publication.
  • Winners are selected by an eSKI judging panel on creativity (40%), storytelling (30%) and image quality (30%). This is a contest of skill, not a lottery.
  • By entering you grant eSKI a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display your entry on eSKI.com and its social channels with credit.
  • Prizes are non-transferable; cash alternatives may be offered at eSKI's discretion. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  • Winners will be notified by email and must respond within 14 days.
  • Sponsors, if any, will be named on this page.
