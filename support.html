Where the money goes

  • Operations: data feeds, hosting add-ons, security and tools.

  • Contributors: paying writers, reporters and photographers fairly.
  • Contests & prizes: rewarding the community.
  • Promotion: reaching more skiers so the tools improve for everyone.

Contributions to eSKI are not tax-deductible charitable donations. They support an independent publication.
