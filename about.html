About eSKI

An independent, ad-supported ski website built to answer the three questions every skier asks: where is it snowing, where should I go, and what will it cost?

Why eSKI exists

Planning a ski trip usually means juggling a weather app, three resort websites, a pass provider, a lodging site and a spreadsheet. eSKI puts the decision-making pieces in one place: live forecast rankings, side-by-side resort data, an honest budget calculator and plain-English guides.

What we believe

  • Data first. Forecasts come from transparent public weather models and are refreshed automatically.
  • Independent. We are not owned by a resort or pass company. Sponsors never influence rankings.
  • Free for skiers. eSKI is funded by advertising, partnerships and reader support.
  • Safety matters. We point you to official avalanche forecasts and professional instruction.

The name

"eSKI" simply means electronic ski: skiing, online. eSKI is not connected to any other company, product or trademark that uses the letters E-S-K-I. See our trademark and copyright disclosure.
